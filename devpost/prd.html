MindSync PRD

Detailed Journey, Surfaces, Behaviors & Acceptance Criteria

📱 Surfaces & Navigation

MindSync is structured into four dedicated, clean surfaces:

💬 Chat Workspace
📚 History (Sort/Filter)
☁️ Topics & Word Cloud
⚙️ Settings (Keys/Ollama)
  • Chat: Focused, distraction-free conversation area with live model switching.
  • History View: Standalone surface with sorting (Date, Title, Message Count) and filtering (by Topic, Model, search query).
  • Topics & Insights: Word Cloud (starts blank, weights words by frequency) + instant multi-chat synthesis on click.
  • Settings: BYO API keys (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic) & local Ollama endpoint configuration.
💬 Comments on Surfaces & Layout:

🧠 Natural Memory Recall Experience

When asking "you mentioned X previously, can you remind me what you said?":

  • ✓ The assistant incorporates past knowledge naturally into conversational dialogue.
  • ✓ A discreet citation tag appears alongside the message (e.g. [🧠 Ref: Sep 24 • Architecture Chat]).
  • ✓ Clicking the citation expands to inspect the exact historical snippet, with a "Jump to Conversation" button to open the full past chat.
💬 Comments on Memory Recall:

☁️ Word Cloud & Multi-Chat Synthesis

  • ✓ Starts blank upon initial fresh launch, populating dynamically as conversations are recorded.
  • ✓ Word sizes dynamically scale based on term frequency and relevance across chats.
  • ✓ Clicking a word filters conversations discussing that topic and generates an aggregated AI synthesis summary.
