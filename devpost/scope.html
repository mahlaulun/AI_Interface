🎯 Who It's For & "Working" Demo

User: Personal second brain for power users chatting with multiple models across laptop and mobile.

1-Minute Demo Finish Line:

  • Connect API key or Ollama.
  • Chat across 2-3 distinct subjects (e.g. system design, recipes, reading notes).
  • Open Dashboard to view interactive Word Cloud. Click a topic to see an instant multi-chat summary.
  • Ask a recall question ("What did we discuss earlier regarding X?"); observe the verified snippet card + accurate answer.
